# Why math needs a stage
Meaning in math begins with our own questions.

They lead us down unknown paths, with only our curiosity to guide us.

And when we find our own way through, those questions become stories worth sharing.

That’s why we need a stage to bring math to life


# The stage takes on many forms
Math Symposium

Students presenting their coded model of a torus to their class
Investigating the torus behind Changi’s Jewel · Temasek Junior College
Students present mathematical investigations through animations and interactive models, inviting the audience to follow their reasoning and join the discussion.

Focus: Mathematical inquiry


Math Festival

A student telling a story connecting earthquakes and sinusoidal waves into a microphone
A story of earthquakes and sinusoidal waves · Nemasu Senior Secondary School
Students share mathematical films, artworks and stories, inviting the audience to watch screenings, explore exhibits and meet the creators.

Focus: Creative expression


Student-led Workshops

Student leaders explaining functions and composition beside a projected animation
The mathematics behind an animated face · St Patrick’s Secondary School
Students guide peers or juniors through PaperLand workshops, helping participants explore mathematical ideas and create animations of their own.

Focus: Student leadership
