{%- comment -%} Small decorative UI icons. Paths, rather than font glyphs, keep the shape consistent across platforms. The enclosing button/link supplies its label. Params: name (play, asterisk, arrow-up, arrow-up-right, expand, close, github, youtube). The two marks at the end are NOT drawn here and must not be: they are the publishers' own geometry, carried verbatim (the repository's 24x24 mark as GitHub publishes it; YouTube's from simple-icons, CC0) so that a link that leaves the site wears the shape its destination already has. Redrawing them would be inventing brand geometry — the same line design/media.org draws around the wordmark's raster pieces. What the site owns is how they are lit: components/helios.css, "the close's other doors". {%- endcomment -%}
