          <p>We plan around what you want your students to achieve. A simple show-and-tell can take as few as four training sessions, followed by one session for students to share their work.</p>
        </details>
        <details name="experiences-planning">
          <summary>What support does PaperLand provide?</summary>
          <p>We provide the computational platform, design the programme with you, and train your students or teachers to bring it to life.</p>

          <summary>How does this fit our maths curriculum?</summary>
