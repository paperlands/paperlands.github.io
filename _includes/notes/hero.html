{%- comment -%} The hero image — the note's own art, in the opening's art slot. This is a placeholder element, not a decoration: the study's opening drew a plate here, and that plate was how a HERO IMAGE is supposed to look, not content to keep. The slot takes the note's image and stands it the way the study stood its art: high right, behind the head, dissolving into the paper. Params: note the note document (for its defaults) image the art. Default: note.heroimgurl — every note carries one alt description. Default: note.hero_alt; empty means decorative With no image the element renders nothing — the slot is a placeholder, and an empty one is honest. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign hero_image = include.image | default: include.note.heroimgurl -%} {%- if hero_image %}
