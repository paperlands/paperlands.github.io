Thinking by building

Freedom to Experiment

What happens if Fibonacci adds the 2nd and 3rd most recent terms instead? Challenge the rule, see what happens, and don't take the formula for granted.

Unlimited Creativity

How can I make my tree more lifelike? Modelling the world around us invites students to notice, use and reshape the mathematics hidden within it.

Students shouldn't have to wait until university to experience mathematics as something they can explore, build and create with. PaperLand is making computational mathematical exploration accessible much earlier.
