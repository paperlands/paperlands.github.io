Designing Math Experiences

FAQ

How much preparation is needed?

We plan around what you want your students to achieve. A simple show-and-tell can take as few as four training sessions, followed by one session for students to share their work.

What support does PaperLand provide?

We provide the computational platform, design the programme with you, and train your students or teachers to bring it to life.

How does this fit our maths curriculum?

Our lessons build on familiar middle and high school maths topics. Browse our lesson plans to see what students can explore. We’ll help you adapt them to your curriculum.

What equipment do we need?

All students need are devices. If there are not enough to go around, they can work in pairs and groups.
